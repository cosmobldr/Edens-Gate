3D Model Shrinker

Drop in a .glb file and get it back much smaller, looking the same. Smaller models load faster in games and on websites. It all happens on your device: your file is never uploaded.

Download

How it works

Most of a 3D file's size is its pictures (textures). This tool scales oversized pictures down, saves them in a smaller format (WebP), removes duplicate data, and packs the shape itself with Meshopt compression. Names, materials and animations stay as they are.

Only .glb files for now. Have a .gltf, .fbx or .obj? Open it in Blender (free) and export as glTF Binary (.glb) first.
