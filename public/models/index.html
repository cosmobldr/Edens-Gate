Free 3D Models

Made to be given away.

Every model here is free to download and use for anything: games, films, renders, apps, school, work. Each one comes as a GLB for the web and game engines (Unity, Unreal, Godot) and as its original Blender file. No sign-up. No credit required.

      What you can do with them

      All models in The Open Asset Forge are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 by A. Penn & Co. Use them in commercial projects, modify them, sell what you make with them. You don't need permission and you don't need to give credit, though a mention is always welcome.
