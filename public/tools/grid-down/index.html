Print it while the printer still works.

The best free emergency guides, gathered in one place. Every one comes straight from FEMA, Ready.gov or the CDC: official U.S. government guides, free for anyone to print and share. Eden's Gate didn't write them; we just put them where you can find them.

  • ⎙ Family Emergency Communication PlanReady.gov · fillable Phone numbers, meeting places and who to call if you get separated. Fill it in and keep copies.↗
  • ⎙ Emergency plan wallet cardsReady.gov Pocket-size cards for each family member to carry.↗

Make it count

A PDF on a dead phone doesn't help. Print a copy of each, put them in a folder or zip bag with your kit, and fill in the family plan together. Then add music for the long nights: build a Survival Radio, or save songs to play with no signal in the vault.

Links go to the official sites and were checked in November 2026. If one has moved, search the title on ready.gov or cdc.gov.
