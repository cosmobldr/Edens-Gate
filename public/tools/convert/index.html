    Good to know

    Converting an MP3 to WAV makes it easier to edit, but it can't add back quality the MP3 already lost. For the best result, keep your original WAV and make MP3s from it.

    Very long files (over an hour) can take a minute and need plenty of memory. Phones may struggle with those; a computer handles them fine.
