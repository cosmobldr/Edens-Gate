Good to know

Loudness is measured the way streaming services do it (integrated LUFS, ITU-R BS.1770). Spotify, YouTube and Amazon aim for about −14 LUFS and turn louder songs down. Apple Music aims for about −16. Peak shows the highest sample in the file.

Tempo and key are a computer's best guess. They're usually right, but tempo can come out half or double, and songs that change key can confuse it. Trust your ears.
