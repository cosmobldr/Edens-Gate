Free for the Classroom

Bookmark this page. Everything is free, always.

No sign-up, no student accounts, no ads. It all runs in a web browser, Chromebooks included, and the music and 3D models are public domain (CC0): students can use, remix and publish them without asking.

Art, math and quiet time

  • ✎ Printable coloring pagesMandalas, optical illusions, stained glass, star tiles and mazes. Print one page or a pack of ten, a new set every time.In class: mandalas and star tiles for symmetry and tessellation, illusions to talk about how the eye is fooled, a calm-down page for early finishers.→

STEM, 3D and game design

  • ❖ 2,000+ free 3D modelsEveryday objects in 14 categories, from kitchen and garden to science lab and music studio, as GLB and Blender files. Spin any of them in 3D right in the browser.In class: build a scene in Blender, drop props into a Godot or Unity game, measure and compare real objects in 3D, or 3D-print one.→
  • ⌂ Browse by room14 categories, from Home & Living to Health & Science, each on its own page.In class: a scavenger hunt: find five objects and describe what each one is made of.→
  • ◆ 3D model shrinkerMakes a 3D file up to 10× smaller without changing how it looks.In class: why file size matters on the web, and what a texture is.→
  • ☀ Build a solar radioParts list, wiring, 3D-printable cases and wooden box plans for a radio that runs on sunlight.In class: a hands-on electronics and solar-power project for a club or an older class.→

Music class

  • ♩ Tuner and metronomeTune guitar, ukulele, violin or bass with the mic, plus a metronome with tap tempo.In class: keep a whole room in tune, practice steady tempo, show what a beat per minute is.→
  • ◎ Song CheckFinds a song's tempo (BPM) and musical key, and how loud it is.In class: guess the tempo, then check it; find the key of a favorite song.→
  • ✂ Song trimmerCut a song to just the part you need, with a fade, as MP3 or WAV.In class: trim backing tracks for a concert, recital or dance.→

Student videos and projects

  • ▣ Cover and poster makerType a title, pick a look, download artwork for an album, a YouTube thumbnail or a Story.In class: project title cards and covers for class podcasts or albums.→

Safety and preparedness

  • ⎙ Emergency guidesOfficial FEMA, Ready.gov and CDC guides in one place: family plan, supply list, safe water and first aid.In class: a family emergency plan as homework, or a preparedness unit with printable handouts.→

Good to know

Public domain means no strings. The 3D models and music are released under CC0: no credit needed, no license to track, fine for school, the yearbook or a student's portfolio.

Preview music first. The song vault is for everyone, not just kids, and a few songs have adult language. For a ready-to-play classroom stream, Alec Penn Radio has a Kids Mode that cuts out swear words.

Nothing is uploaded. The tools work on files right on the device, so student work stays on the school's machines.
